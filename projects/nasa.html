🪐 Mission Overview

During the NASA L'SPACE Mission Concept Academy, I worked with Team 18 to develop Aeris, a conceptual mission designed to explore Venus. Our mission proposed using a robotic balloon, called an aerobot, to study Venus's atmosphere and investigate possible volcanic activity.

👩‍🚀 My Role: Scientist 1

As Scientist 1, my responsibilities focused on mission planning and scientific data analysis. I researched scientific instruments, explored methods for collecting atmospheric measurements, and contributed to the mission's proposed data-collection plan.

🔬 Scientific Instruments

Our team proposed using scientific instruments to study the composition of Venus's atmosphere and identify signs of volcanic activity.

  • UV-Visible Spectrometer: Used to study atmospheric gases.
  • Thermal Emission Spectrometer: Used to investigate thermal emissions and surface minerals.

📋 Engineering Design Review

Our team developed a Preliminary Design Review (PDR) documenting the proposed mission. The report covered scientific objectives, spacecraft systems, engineering requirements, mission risks, and planning.
