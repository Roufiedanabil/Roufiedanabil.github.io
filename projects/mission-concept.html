🪐 About the Mission

During the NASA L'SPACE Mission Concept Academy, I worked with Team 18 to develop Aeris, a conceptual robotic balloon mission designed to explore Venus. Our team investigated how atmospheric measurements and thermal observations could help scientists study volcanic activity.

👩‍🚀 My Role: Scientist 1

As Scientist 1, I was responsible for mission planning and data analysis. My scientific work included researching instruments and developing a proposed sequence for collecting mission data.

🔬 Scientific Instruments

UV-Visible Spectrometer

Our proposed UV-Visible Spectrometer would study atmospheric gases, including carbonyl sulfide (OCS).

Thermal Emission Spectrometer

Our proposed thermal instrument would help investigate surface minerals and possible volcanic activity on Venus.

📡 Mission Data Collection

I explored a proposed 48-hour observation plan that included instrument stabilization, atmospheric gas scanning, thermal observations, and transmission of scientific data.

📋 Preliminary Design Review

Our team prepared a Preliminary Design Review (PDR) covering scientific objectives, mission requirements, spacecraft systems, risks, and engineering planning.

The team report also documents my Scientist 1 role on page 25.
